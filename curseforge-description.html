01 / THE EXPERIENCE

The rules are familiar. The world isn't.

You still mine, craft, build, explore, fight and try to make it home. But in Still Watching, an ordinary journey can become a terrible idea. The weather closes in. Your footsteps echo. Something moves beyond your view distance. A reassuring light may be farther away than it looks.

This pack builds fear through atmosphere, stalking threats, sound design and exploration—not simply through the number of monsters it can fit into a cave.

HEAR THE DANGER

Ambient sound, footsteps and spatial audio make enclosed spaces and distant movement matter.

FEAR THE UNKNOWN

Fog, darkness and hostile encounters turn routine travel into calculated risk.

SURVIVE TOGETHER

Maps, equipment, quests and voice chat support tense private multiplayer sessions.

02 / HORROR & ATMOSPHERE

You may hear it before you see it.

AmbientSounds 6, Sound Physics Remastered, Presence Footsteps and Cave Dweller Sound Overhaul give the landscape an uneasy soundtrack. EnhancedVisuals, Fresh Animations and other visual resources strengthen the sense that Minecraft is not behaving as it should.

The threat roster includes The One Who Watches, The Anomaly, The Man From The Fog, From The Fog, Cave Dweller Reimagined, The Knocker, The Mimicer, The Midnight Lurker, GoatMan, Siren Head and more. Their encounters create different reasons to question a noise, a shadow or an apparently empty path.

"A closed door is not the same thing as safety."

Headphones are recommended. Directional sound is part of the experience.

04 / TOOLS, SURVIVAL & FRIENDS

Preparation helps. It does not guarantee a return.

The pack adds useful ways to travel, defend yourself, recover from mistakes and coordinate with friends. These systems support the survival loop without removing the reason to fear the dark.

  • Navigate: Xaero's Minimap and Xaero's World Map help keep track of the world.
  • Fight: Timeless and Classics Zero Guns expands weapon options; TACZ: Durability introduces wear and upkeep.
  • Recover and defend: Traveler's Backpack, Corpse and Security Craft provide practical survival tools.
  • Play as a group: Simple Voice Chat, FTB Teams and FTB Quests support communication and shared progression.

For dedicated servers, use the exact matching pack release, Forge build, required configuration and world-generation files. See the server setup guide before attempting manual deployment.

05 / VISUALS & PERFORMANCE

Atmosphere is essential. Shaders aren't.

FIVE SHADER CHOICES

Complementary Reimagined, Complementary Unbound, Hysteria, Insanity and Spooklementary offer different ways to shape the atmosphere.

They are included, not required.

PRACTICAL FOUNDATIONS

Embeddium, Chloride, ImmediatelyFast, FerriteCore, ModernFix and FPS Reducer form the pack's rendering and resource-efficiency foundation.

No universal FPS promise: hardware, shaders, view distance and new terrain generation still matter.

Recommended starting point: 5 GB allocated RAM minimum, 6–8 GB preferred where system memory permits; Java 17 when needed; 8–10 render-distance chunks if performance is uncertain. Lower or disable shaders first when troubleshooting frame rate.

06 / START PLAYING

Go in prepared.

  1. Open the official Still Watching CurseForge page or find the pack in the CurseForge App.
  2. Confirm the creator is Soumyajit and select the intended Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge release.
  3. Install to a clean profile. Allocate at least 5 GB RAM, or 6–8 GB where appropriate.
  4. Launch without extra mods, shaders, resource packs or copied old configurations.
  5. Create a new test world. Check world generation, controls, sound, maps and performance before investing in a long-term save.

Important: The GitHub repository ZIP is source/documentation material, not the playable CurseForge installer.

07 / WORLD SAFETY

Do not gamble your old world.

V2.0.0 is a major generation and mapping overhaul, not a drop-in update to V1.1.2.

  • Back up the entire old instance and every important world.
  • Install V2.0.0 in a fresh CurseForge profile and make a new world.
  • Do not copy the entire V1.1.2 config/ folder over the new installation.
  • Preserve legacy JourneyMap data separately. It does not automatically transfer to Xaero's maps.
  • If an existing save must be tested, work from a disposable copy.
